The Product Wedge

Presence over Volume: Only candidates currently active and present are displayed. Recruiters see real intent, not cold lists.
Anonymized until Mutual: Name and face are locked and protected, preventing screening bias, and revealing only at the moment of connection.
Participation-Free Signal: Provenance from work-email verification and connected third-party accounts replaces easily-fabricated resumes.
