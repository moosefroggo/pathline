Senior talent is quietly open.

The best candidates often cannot publicly signal open to work without creating career, reputation, or employer risk.

Pathline is the private open-to-work layer for senior talent.

Identity lock is not a flourish. It is the mechanism that creates supply.

Trust

Trust builds in layers.

Pathline does not ask recruiters to trust another fake-polished resume. It composes enough provenance, intent, and presence to start.

Facts

Work email, connected links, source tags.

Protection

Name and face reveal only after mutual choice.

Intent

Role, stage, comp, and timing as structured chips.

Liveness is one layer, not proof. Deeper identity and background checks stay downstream.

Business

Sell private access to readiness.

Buyer

Talent leaders hiring senior, sensitive, specialized roles.

Pilot

Six to eight weeks across roles where passive supply matters.

Pricing

Role-based subscription once qualified moments convert.

Measure private windows opened, qualified first conversations, booked follow-ups, and recruiter confidence.

Risks

The hard parts are clear.

Supply
Will senior candidates open private windows often enough?
Trust
Will recruiters act before seeing name, face, or full resume?
Fraud
Verified facts and liveness raise trust, but cannot carry the full burden.
Fit
The product has to sit cleanly before the ATS and deeper checks.

The next step is to test the two riskiest assumptions: candidate consent to private availability and recruiter willingness to act on protected identity.
