Visual directions

Two palettes, one principle: not LinkedIn blue.

Both directions chase warmth and presence over cold efficiency. We pressure-tested a soft warm palette, then pushed to something more distinctive and modern.

Product concepts

A wide search for a genuinely different shape.

We brainstormed many shapes for "the moment," and pushed on each — most collapsed into "a moment with a tweak," drifted toward evaluation, or recreated the funnel we set out to kill.

Meet-Live · the momentShipped
Presence board → anonymized brief → 2-min live moment → identity reveal.
The core. Presence + consent, mobile-native.
Solve-TogetherBuilt
Work a real slice of the role together for 15 min; fit shows itself.
Deeper-stage alt. Drifts to evaluation · desktop-leaning · higher friction.
Office-hours roomSet aside
Candidate opens a drop-in window; recruiters join.
Just a moment with a lobby.
Drop-into-the-teamSet aside
Candidate sits in on a real team working session.
Desktop-bound; high friction; voyeuristic.
Speed-roundsSet aside
Back-to-back timed mini-meets, speed-dating style.
"A moment with extra time," not a new shape.
Reverse-auctionSet aside
Employers bid for a candidate's time/attention.
Turns connection into a marketplace transaction.
TikTok-duetSet aside
Candidate clips + recruiter response clips.
Back to authored media — the thing AI killed.
Voice-notes / problem-first feedSet aside
Async voice or a feed of role problems to react to.
Re-introduces asynchrony — the void's home.
"AI, pull me candidates"Rejected
Natural-language sourcing that returns a ranked list.
D-001. A list to skim = the funnel + the AI-gatekeeper dystopia.
Behavior analyzer (rank-the-best)Rejected
Score candidates on proof-of-work, surface the strongest.
A different product (analysis, not connection); evaluation is the enemy.

Element & interaction variations

What changed at the component level.

The decisions inside the screens — each one moved from a first pass to the shipped treatment.

Solve-Together layout
2 static phones, side by side→one clickable, animated flow
Brought the explored alternative to parity with the core prototype.
"Working it out together" thread
bulleted dot-list of lines→chat bubbles (you right / them left)
A transcript didn't read as a conversation; bubbles do.
Primary button
coral fill, white text→yellow fill, dark ink, hard shadow
White-on-yellow failed contrast; dark-on-yellow is the neo-brutalist signature.
Cards & surfaces
soft rounded, gentle shadow→flat black border + offset shadow
Gives the UI a crafted, intentional, un-templated feel.
Type pairing
Inter + Fraunces→Outfit + Fraunces
Outfit's geometry pairs better with the bold neo-brutalist surfaces.
Identity
locked / anonymized avatar→portrait + Fraunces name at reveal
Anonymized until mutual makes the reveal the emotional climax.
The reveal moment
glow orb + live waveform + name drop
Motion carries the peak — the moment is the hero.
Presence & perishability
live countdown+"window closed" expired state
A moment can pass — scarcity makes presence feel real.
