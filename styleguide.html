Preferred visual strategy

Hidden Gem makes hiring feel quietly alive.

Pathline should not look like a recruiter dashboard, an AI sourcing tool, or a social network. The Hidden Gem route gives the product a premium, private, almost editorial calm: deep sage as trust, warm cream as humanity, and soft live color only when a real moment is opening.

North star
Qualified moments resolved into connection or closure.

The metric reinforces the mission: no void. Success is not more profiles viewed, more messages sent, or more candidates ranked. It is a serious hiring interaction that becomes human and accountable.

Mission
End the hiring void.

Every serious opening should lead to a real conversation or respectful closure within a promised window.

Brand feeling
Private, warm, present.

The tone should feel like being invited into a meaningful room, not dropped into a searchable database.

Design test
Does this reduce the funnel?

Every screen should make the moment feel more human, more consented, and less like a list to judge.
