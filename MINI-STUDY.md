# Mini Gut-Check — Pathline (single link, ideas only)

A fast concept gut-check. **One Maze link.** Tests the **ideas in words** — not the screens — so it
measures whether the *concepts* resonate with recruiters, in ~2–3 minutes. **6 questions.**

> Why ideas-only: a gut check isolates the *concept* from the visual craft. No prototype, no clicking
> — just "does this idea land?" Good for a quick directional read; pair it with the clickable test
> later if you want usability signal too.

Build in Maze: **Create maze → Start from scratch**, add blocks top-to-bottom. **No website blocks
needed** — it's all text.

---

## BLOCK 1 — Welcome screen
> **Two quick ideas for a hiring app — I'd love your gut reaction.**
> ~2 minutes, no right answers. React fast; first instinct is what we're after.

*Button: "Start"*

---

## BLOCK 2 — Multiple choice  *(screener · single-select)*
**Are you involved in hiring or recruiting?**
- Yes — recruiter / talent
- Yes — I hire for my own team (manager / founder)
- No

---

## BLOCK 3 — Context
> Recruiters drown in low-signal applications and candidates get ghosted — the first real conversation rarely happens. Here are two different ideas for fixing that. Just react.

---

## BLOCK 4 — Context  *(Idea A)*
> **Idea A — Meet-Live**
> A hiring app where you only see candidates who are **active and open to talking right now**. Profiles are anonymized — no résumés, no cold outreach. When you're both around, you start a **2-minute live moment**, and identity is revealed only once you both show up.

## BLOCK 5 — Opinion scale  *(1–5)*
> As an idea, how appealing is this?
*Left: "Not for me" · Right: "Love it"*

## BLOCK 6 — Open question  *(required)*
> Gut reaction in a line — what works, what worries you?

---

## BLOCK 7 — Context  *(Idea B)*
> **Idea B — Solve-Together**
> Instead of an interview, you and a candidate spend **15 minutes working a real problem from the role together**, live. No grading, no take-home — you just see how they actually think, and fit shows itself.

## BLOCK 8 — Opinion scale  *(1–5)*
> As an idea, how appealing is this?
*Left: "Not for me" · Right: "Love it"*

## BLOCK 9 — Open question  *(required)*
> Gut reaction in a line — what works, what worries you?

---

## BLOCK 10 — Multiple choice  *(single-select · required)*
**Which idea is stronger?**
- Meet-Live
- Solve-Together
- Neither

## BLOCK 11 — Open question  *(required)*
> Why?

---

# Run it

**Recruit (free):** Maze → **Share → copy link** → DM real recruiters. Aim for **6–10**. One link, no counterbalancing to worry about.

**Ready-to-send DM:**
> Hi [name] — got 2 minutes for a gut check? Two ideas for a recruiter hiring tool, I just want your honest first reaction. No signup. [link] 🙏

**Read it:** Maze averages the two 1–5 scores + the "which is stronger" split. The gold is the **open-line reactions** — tally the recurring worry (watch for *"will anyone actually be live/present?"* on Meet-Live).

**Honest caveat for the deck:** ideas-only + small n → this measures **concept resonance**, not usability or visual appeal. Directional, by design.
