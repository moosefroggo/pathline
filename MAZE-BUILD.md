## BLOCK 3 — Context
> Recruiters drown in low-signal applications and candidates get ghosted. **Pathline is a mobile app for the recruiter side** — it's not about a faster funnel, it's about fixing the *first real conversation* between a recruiter and a candidate. Here are two ideas for doing that.

# After you build it

## Counterbalance the order (do once)
1. **Duplicate the maze.**
2. In the copy, move the **Solve-Together** group (blocks 9–13) *above* the **Meet-Live** group (blocks 4–8).
3. You now have an **A-first** and **B-first** maze. Send half your recruits each link — cancels order bias.

## Recruit (free — bring your own participants)
**Maze → Share → copy link.** Send to real recruiters. Aim **6–10** (≈half each link).
Where: LinkedIn DMs, recruiting Slack/Discord (e.g. RecruitingBrainfood), your network.

**Ready-to-send DM:**
> Hi [name] — I'm pressure-testing two concepts for a recruiter hiring tool and would love a recruiter's gut read. ~4 min, on your phone, anonymous, no signup. Mind clicking through? [link] 🙏

## Launch & read
- **Publish**, send links, watch responses in the dashboard.
- Maze aggregates the two 1–5 scales + the "which would you use" split automatically. Read the **open answers by hand** — does the comprehension match the intent, and what worry recurs? (Watch for the *"will anyone actually be live / present?"* liquidity worry — that's your best deck slide.)

## Pre-launch checklist
- [ ] Both **Website testing** blocks open the right URL and let you click around.
- [ ] Required on: blocks 6, 8, 11, 13, 14, 15.
- [ ] Opinion-scale labels set (not useful ↔ very useful).
- [ ] Made the duplicate with reversed concept order.
- [ ] Tested it once on your own phone.

## If the embedded site misbehaves
Our prototypes set no frame-blocking headers, so they load fine in Maze. If the JS ever stalls inside Maze's frame, swap each **Website testing** block for a **Context block with the link** ("open this, click through, come back") — same data, zero risk.
